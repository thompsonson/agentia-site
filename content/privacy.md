**Last updated: January 18, 2025**

This privacy policy describes how Le Stash ("the Application") handles your data.

## What is Le Stash?

Le Stash is a personal application that allows you to download and archive your own LinkedIn content, including your posts, reactions, and comments.

## Data We Access

When you authorize Le Stash with your LinkedIn account, the Application accesses:

- Your LinkedIn posts and articles
- Your reactions (likes, celebrations, etc.)
- Your comments on posts

This data is accessed solely through LinkedIn's official API with your explicit authorization.

## How Your Data is Used

Your data is used exclusively to:

- Download and export your own content for personal archival purposes

The Application does **not**:

- Store your data on external servers
- Share your data with third parties
- Use your data for analytics or advertising
- Retain your data after export

## Data Storage

All downloaded content is stored locally on your own device. The Application does not maintain any external database of user content.

## Your Control

You can revoke Le Stash's access to your LinkedIn account at any time through your [LinkedIn settings](https://www.linkedin.com/psettings/permitted-services).
